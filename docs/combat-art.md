# Combat art

The arena, tag-team effect, and new cast artwork use the built-in ImageGen tool. The website and standalone demo share the same assets.

`public/abilities/duel-street.webp`

```text

Draw a static exterior in the tactile, deliberately simple late-1990s construction-paper cutout look of South Park: The Stick of Truth, with hard-edged imperfect paper shapes, a subtle matte paper grain, flat solid fills, sparse detail, and an orthographic TV-animation camera. A dark charcoal two-lane street crosses the entire width, with the near black asphalt clean and unobstructed for the bottom 43% of the image where the two game characters will stand and animate. Along its far edge is a narrow irregular white snowbank and a gray sidewalk. Behind the sidewalk, low wooden storefronts and two colorful blocky residential buildings, evergreen trees, and layers of forest-green foothills under a pale winter sky. Most building details should be concentrated above the horizontal middle of the frame. Low camera is level and side-on, street edge horizontal, not an overhead map and not a street vanishing into the distance. Strong broad blocks of forest green, russet and mustard, white snow, nearly black asphalt. Composed for clean gameplay silhouettes on both halves of the street, including when center cropped on a phone. Background only: no people, no cars, no UI, no titles, no readable signage, no logos, no glossy gradients, no bevels, no 3D render, no lens effects.
```
